Background & Academic Vision

About Me

Welcome! I am an Assistant Professor of Information Technology dedicated to fostering technical excellence, scientific inquiry, and critical thinking among undergraduate and postgraduate computer science students at B.C.S. Government Post Graduate College.

My academic journey centers around transforming theoretical computer science foundations into real-world technological solutions. Alongside university lectures in programming languages, databases, and core software principles, I actively investigate how frontier Artificial Intelligence can be democratized for societal benefit.

My core research explores Multi-Agent Memory Architecture, Generative LLM orchestration, and Healthcare Diagnostic Evaluation—specifically comparing deterministic clinical heuristics against deep neural diagnostic models to improve interpretability and trust.

Instruction Philosophy
